### Who we are

Example Org runs about 2,000 Windows and Linux endpoints across a dozen
offices, with most servers in one datacenter. Treat anything internet-facing
as more urgent than the same finding on an internal host.

### Escalating

- A confirmed exploited vulnerability on an internet-facing host is a P1:
  alert the operator at once, any hour.
- Everything else waits for the morning summary unless it has more than 50
  hosts.
- Name the owning team when you know it: endpoints belong to the desktop
  team, servers to infrastructure, network gear to the network team.

### This quarter

The browser and VPN client fleet is being standardized. Findings in either
are worth a line in the summary even when they are not exploited.
