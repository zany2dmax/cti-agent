# /handoff-note

Write one board message to `@operator`, kind `INFO`, of at most five lines:

1. Anything open on the board marked P1 or Q, and who it waits on.
2. Any lane whose last run failed or was skipped this week.
3. Anything due before Monday (an overdue ticket, an expiring credential).

If there is nothing in any of the three, write "Nothing open for the
weekend." and stop. Do not repeat the week's findings: the Daily Brief has
them.
